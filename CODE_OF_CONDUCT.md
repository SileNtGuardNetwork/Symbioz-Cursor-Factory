# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, review evidence rather than personalities, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- providing reproducible reports and specific feedback
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds
- disclosing uncertainty and limitations
- protecting private data, credentials, and customer information
- accepting maintainer decisions on scope and security boundaries

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- publishing private information or credentials
- intentionally bypassing safety controls
- submitting malicious code, poisoned prompts, or deceptive MCP configurations
- repeatedly expanding discussions beyond the documented project scope

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior threatens contributors, users, or project security.

Security concerns should be reported through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through public disclosure.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other public spaces representing the project.
